About Wasatch Growth Group

A family-run growth partner for local home-service contractors, starting with fencing.

Who we are

Wasatch Growth Group is a family company, started by brothers Jackson and Noah. We work the way a good family business does: we answer quickly, we do what we say, and we treat every contractor we work with like a long-term partner.

Co-founder · Contractor partnerships

Our mission

Wasatch Growth Group exists to help hardworking business owners grow through high-quality customer connections and build meaningful partnerships.

Why we focus on quality

Too many lead programs push volume. We built our services around a different idea: It’s not about the quantity, it’s about the quality. More qualified opportunities. Less wasted outreach.

Our services are called Streamline Contracting Solutions, a Contractor Growth System. It is how our team runs the work behind the scenes so your team can focus on estimates and jobs.

How we work with you

We deliver qualified homeowner opportunities as a service, not software you have to learn. Our team organizes the opportunity. Your team executes. You keep your process, your brand, and your relationship with the homeowner. See the four steps.

A partnership, not a marketplace

We do not operate like shared-lead marketplaces that send the same homeowner to 3 or 4 pros. Each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold.

Behind the name

Our family has deep roots in Utah, and the Wasatch Range is part of where we come from. We named the company after those mountains because they stand for what we want to build with our partners: something steady, built to last, and always climbing.

That is the idea in our logo, too. The mountain forms the W in Wasatch, and the arrow rising from it stands for the growth we work toward with every contractor.

Let’s talk about your business

If you own a contracting business and want to grow with fewer dead ends, we would like to hear from you. You will talk with one of us directly.
