About Wasatch Growth Group

A growth partner for local home-service contractors, starting with fencing.

Our mission

Wasatch Growth Group exists to help hardworking business owners grow through high-quality customer connections and build meaningful partnerships.

Why we focus on quality

Too many lead programs push volume. We built our services around a different idea: It’s not about the quantity, it’s about the quality. More qualified opportunities. Less wasted outreach.

Our services are called Streamline Contracting Solutions, a Contractor Growth System. It runs on three parts: Optimize, Qualify, Execute.

How we work with you

We run an AI-powered lead service, not software you have to learn. Our team organizes the opportunity. Your team executes. You keep your process, your brand, and your relationship with the homeowner. See the four steps.

A partnership, not a marketplace

We do not operate like shared-lead marketplaces that send the same homeowner to 3 or 4 pros. Each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold. Pricing is a flat monthly rate, not pay-per-lead, so the partnership stays predictable.

Let’s talk about your business

If you own a contracting business and want to grow with fewer dead ends, we would like to hear from you.
