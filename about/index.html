About Wasatch Growth Group

We are a growth partner for local home-service contractors.

Wasatch Growth Group exists to help hardworking business owners grow through high-quality customer connections and build meaningful partnerships.

Too many lead programs push volume. We built Streamline Contracting Solutions—a Contractor Growth System—around a different idea: It’s not about the quantity, it’s about the quality. More qualified opportunities. Less wasted outreach.

How we work with you

We run an AI-powered lead service, not software you have to learn. Our team organizes the opportunity. Your team executes. You keep your process, your brand, and your relationship with the homeowner.

Partnership, not a marketplace pile-on

We do not operate like shared-lead marketplaces that send the same homeowner to 3 or 4 pros. Each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold. Pricing is a flat monthly rate framed as a predictable partnership—not pay-per-lead.
