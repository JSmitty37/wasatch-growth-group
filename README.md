# wasatchgrowthgroup.com

Contractor-facing site for Wasatch Growth Group. Plain HTML and CSS, served by GitHub Pages from `main`.

## Pages
- `/` Home, `/how-it-works/`, `/about/`, `/book-a-call/`, `/privacy/`, plus `404.html`
- Shared styles: `styles.css`. Logos and the share image: `assets/`. Favicons at the root.

## Editing notes
- Header, footer, and the mobile "Book a call" bar are repeated on every page. Change all pages together.
- Every "Book a call" button is a `mailto:` link to jackson@wasatchgrowthgroup.com with a subject and a short body template.
- Update `sitemap.xml` `lastmod` when a page's content changes.

## Rules for this site
- Brand name only: "Wasatch Growth Group". No legal entity names, owner names, street addresses, or other emails.
- Make no claims that a territory or lead is reserved or protected for one contractor. The only allowed contrast is the approved line: each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold.
- Never name where leads come from (no ad platforms or homeowner-facing site names).
- No dollar amounts, guarantees, made-up stats, testimonials, client counts, or years in business.
- No form backend, analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies.
- Keep the approved lines word for word: the tagline, subtitle, services name, mission, Detect/Qualify/Deliver/Respond, "Our team organizes the opportunity. Your team executes.", Optimize/Qualify/Execute, and the one-contractor-per-homeowner contrast.
- Don't change `CNAME`.
