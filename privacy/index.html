Privacy

Plain-language privacy for this website. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Wasatch Growth Group operates wasatchgrowthgroup.com. This page explains what information we handle when you use this site.

What we collect

This site has no contact form, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking pixels. If you choose to email us (for example, to book a call), we receive whatever you include in that message, typically your email address, name, and the details you write.

How we use email contact

We use your email only to reply, schedule a conversation, and follow up about a possible partnership. We do not sell your contact information.

Hosting and logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, their infrastructure may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to build marketing profiles.

Cookies

We do not set cookies on this site. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data as it does for any website.

Third parties

If you follow a link away from this site, that site’s privacy practices apply. Our Book a call links open your own email app; the message stays between you and us.

Questions

For privacy questions, email jackson@wasatchgrowthgroup.com.
