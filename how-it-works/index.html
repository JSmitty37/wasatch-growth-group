What’s included

Unlike shared-lead marketplaces, each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold to 3 or 4 pros. Here is what comes with the partnership.

  • Automated lead tracking Every opportunity is logged and followed, so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Multi-platform outreach We reach homeowners across several channels, so you are not relying on just one.
  • Territory optimization We focus on the cities and areas you want to serve and the work you want more of.
  • Dedicated support A real point of contact who knows your business and answers your questions.
  • Ongoing optimization We review what is working and keep adjusting targeting and follow-up over time.
  • Resource library Practical guides and checklists to help your team respond quickly and consistently.

Common questions

Does my team have to learn a new system?

No. It is an AI-powered lead service, not software. Our team organizes the opportunity. Your team executes.

Do you send the same homeowner to other contractors?

No. Unlike shared-lead marketplaces, each homeowner goes to one contractor only and is never resold to 3 or 4 pros.

What trades do you work with?

We are starting with fencing contractors. If you run another kind of home-service business, book a call and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

What does my team need to do?

Respond quickly when an opportunity comes in, run the estimate, and do the work the way you always have. You keep your process, your brand, and the customer relationship.
