Done-for-you Google Business Profile management

When a homeowner searches for a local service, like “plumber near me” or “roofer near me,” Google shows a map with three businesses at the top. A complete, active, well-reviewed profile helps any local service business show up there and look like a shop people can trust. We handle the weekly work on your Google listing so your team can stay on the job.

Why your Google profile matters

Most contractors have a Google Business Profile, but it is easy for it to go stale. Posts stop. Reviews sit unanswered. Hours or services drift out of date. Meanwhile, the shops that keep their profiles current are the ones homeowners see first.

We take that upkeep off your plate. You keep ownership of the profile. We are added as a manager only, and we do the steady work that helps the listing stay accurate, active, and easy to trust.

We do not promise a specific ranking or a spot in Google’s top three results. Rankings depend on many things no one fully controls. What we do is keep the profile in good shape, week after week, and show you plainly how it is performing.

What’s included

Here is what our team handles for you, so yours can stay focused on estimates and jobs.

  • Weekly Google posts Short updates about your work, written in your voice and posted to your profile.
  • Review replies Timely responses to new reviews, including the difficult ones, so no review sits unanswered.
  • Profile monitoring We watch for unexpected edits, suspensions, and verification problems and flag them quickly.
  • Review requests after jobs After each job, we ask the customer for a review, the same way for everyone. You share the customer details, or we plug into the job software you already use.
  • Photo updates We add photos you send us, like finished jobs and your crew at work, so the profile stays current.
  • Map-visibility tracking We check how your profile shows up in Google Maps across your service area, not just from one location.
  • A plain-English monthly report A short recap of profile activity, reviews, and map visibility, written so you can read it in a few minutes.

You keep ownership of your Google Business Profile. We are added as a manager only. We do not buy, fake, or filter reviews: every customer is asked the same way.
